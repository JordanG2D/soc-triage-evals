# Shared egress and log aggregation

A source address may represent many clients behind a proxy or shared service. Determine whether client identifiers, sessions and candidate counts survived aggregation. An approved egress address does not prove each action was approved. If those distinctions are unavailable, preserve the ambiguity and seek client-level records rather than assigning an attack technique from volume alone.
