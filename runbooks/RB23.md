# IP context interpretation

Use IP reputation when the address relationship could change the decision, such as approved service membership or retained control-channel observations. Reputation is contextual evidence, not a verdict. A shared address may hide client identity. Offline fixture entries apply only to their stated scope and time; do not present example addresses as live threat infrastructure.
