# Scheduled maintenance matching

Match a maintenance task to its exact action path, signer, run-as identity, schedule and registration window. A known updater name or SYSTEM principal alone does not establish legitimacy. Explain any mismatch. When an approved change matches all observed scope and no contradictory behavior is retained, document the match without inventing further persistence activity.
