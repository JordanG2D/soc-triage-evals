# Time and scope consistency

Compare event timestamps with authorization windows, fixture as-of times and recovery observations. Do not use later recovery as evidence that the earlier stop was approved. Avoid current-state claims beyond the retained interval. If clocks or windows disagree, state the contradiction and request the source needed to resolve it.
