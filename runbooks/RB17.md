# New location review

Location changes may reflect travel, proxy use or misuse. Ask whether the device is assigned and whether the account owner confirms the session. Registration and a single approved prompt do not establish who operated the device. If travel or owner evidence is unavailable, describe the unresolved alternatives and request the narrow verification needed.
