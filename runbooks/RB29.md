# Claim limits and missing telemetry

Use bounded language for missing execution, absent recovery or unavailable owner confirmation. Missing telemetry is not proof that an action never occurred. Do not invent hashes, geolocation, actor names, stolen contents or later lateral movement. Choose a null technique mapping when the retained evidence does not establish a specific adversarial behavior.
