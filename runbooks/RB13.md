# Backup activity validation

Check the backup job identifier, destination, process, signer, time window and expected size. Encrypted content may limit inspection without making the job suspicious by itself. A matching approved backup record can explain an archive upload. Keep bounded statements such as no recorded remote commands distinct from claims that no other activity occurred anywhere.
