# Escalation and uncertainty

Use needs_escalation when retained evidence cannot distinguish an attack from authorized activity, or a specific review policy requires a human decision. Explain what is unresolved and request the exact missing record. Use true_positive when unauthorized adversarial activity is evidenced and no specific review policy changes the disposition. Use benign only with affirmative evidence matching the activity and its authorization. Escalation does not imply the underlying event is harmless or unknown.
