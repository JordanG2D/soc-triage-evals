# Severity policy

Use severity 1 (critical) for observed restricted-data export from a critical asset or completed credential-memory capture on a critical identity system. Use 2 (high) for evidenced unauthorized remote control, account use, persistence, protection impairment or tool transfer without that critical impact. Use 3 (medium) for unresolved anomalies requiring review. Use 4 (low) for narrowly verified routine authorized activity. State the observed impact; an intimidating rule name is not evidence of impact.
