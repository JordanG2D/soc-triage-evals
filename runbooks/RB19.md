# Security service state review

Check the service stop, startup configuration, initiating process tree, change authorization and sensor heartbeat. Stopping protection and disabling restart outside maintenance supports defense impairment. A missing heartbeat is bounded evidence of visibility loss, not proof of every action during that gap. Retain both the recorded change and the known limits of subsequent visibility.
