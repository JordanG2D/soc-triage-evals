# Protection upgrade recovery

A signed upgrader can legitimately stop a service briefly. Match the exact maintenance window and product, then verify installation of the replacement, running service and resumed sensor heartbeat. A trusted-looking process name or a change ticket alone does not prove recovery. Report the recovery time and do not treat a recovered service as proof that unrelated earlier events were authorized.
