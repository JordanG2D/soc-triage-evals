# Limited process information review

Inspect exact access rights. Limited process-information queries can support normal health monitoring and are not equivalent to reading credential memory. Match the installed product, signed path and configured behavior. Absence of a dump alone is insufficient to dismiss another observed memory-read method. Base the decision on the recorded access, action and authorization.
