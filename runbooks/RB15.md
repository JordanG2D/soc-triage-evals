# Account session ownership

A successful password or MFA check establishes authentication acceptance, not the identity of the operator. Compare device assignment, role, access scope and out-of-band account-owner confirmation. Confirmed unauthorized use of an existing account supports valid-account abuse. Do not infer credential acquisition method or an account subtype that the evidence does not establish.
