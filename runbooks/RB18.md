# Critical asset export review policy

For observed unauthorized restricted-data export from an asset classified critical, assign severity 1 and use needs_escalation so the incident lead can decide response and notification scope. This policy applies even when the underlying attack and technique are well evidenced. Preserve the evidence for data content, control channel, asset classification and missing export authorization. Do not reduce the attack assessment merely because the triage disposition is escalation.
