# Evidence citations

Cite only source identifiers actually available in the run: alert identifiers, returned context identifiers and retrieved runbook identifiers. Attach a citation to the factual claim it supports. A valid identifier does not make an unrelated claim supported. Keep observed facts separate from recommendations and policy interpretations; cite the owning policy when it determines disposition.
