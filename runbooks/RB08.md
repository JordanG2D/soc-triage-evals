# Credential process memory review

Distinguish memory-read rights and a created dump from metadata queries or denied access. Check the source binary path, signature, parent and diagnostic authorization. On systems holding privileged logon sessions, completed unauthorized credential-process capture has critical impact. Do not claim that credentials were extracted or reused unless additional evidence establishes those actions.
